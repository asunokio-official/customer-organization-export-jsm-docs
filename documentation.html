Customer & Organization Export for JSM

Vendor Seisuke Nagahara Version 1.0.0 Contact support@asunoki.jp

Customer & Organization Export for JSM adds one screen to Jira: pick a Jira Service Management project, click Export, and download a CSV of that project’s Customers and Organizations. This page covers how to open it, what each part of the screen does, and how to read the output.

01 Opening the app

  1. From Jira, open the Apps menu in the left sidebar.
  2. Select Customer & Organization Export for JSM under your installed apps.
  3. Choose a project from the Project dropdown.
  4. Click Export CSV.

That’s the entire workflow — there is no configuration screen and nothing to set up beforehand.

02 What happens when you click Export

Your browser calls Jira’s own REST API directly, using your own login session, to read:

  • Every Customer on the selected project (name and email address).
  • Every Organization on the selected project, and which Customers belong to each one.

A progress indicator shows which step is running. When it finishes, your browser immediately downloads a CSV file — nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere else.

03 Reading the CSV

ColumnContents
Customer NameThe Customer’s display name.
EmailThe Customer’s email address, if Jira has one on file.
Organization NameThe name of an Organization the Customer belongs to. Blank if the Customer belongs to no Organization.
Organization IDJira’s internal ID for that Organization. Blank under the same condition as above.

One row per membership, not per Customer. A Customer who belongs to more than one Organization appears on more than one row — once for each Organization — so that no membership is ever dropped to fit a single-value column. A Customer with no Organization still gets exactly one row, with the last two columns left blank.

04 Troubleshooting

  • “この作業項目タイプへのアクセス権限がありません” / a permission error. Your Jira account needs Service Management agent access to the project you selected. The app reads data with your own permissions — it cannot see anything you couldn’t already see in Jira.
  • A rate-limit message. Atlassian temporarily limited how many requests your account can make. Wait the suggested number of seconds (shown in the message, when Atlassian provides one) and try again.
  • An “API仕様が変更された可能性” message. The Customer/Organization endpoints the app uses are marked Experimental by Atlassian, meaning their shape can change without notice. If you see this, please contact support so we can update the app.
  • The export takes a while on a large project. The app reads each Organization’s member list one call at a time (five at once, to stay well under Atlassian’s rate limits). A project with many Organizations will take longer than one with few; there is no hard size limit, only how long you’re willing to wait.
